Further Out: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Further Out ("the app") is a mobile game by Arnau Tresserras ("I", "me"), an individual developer. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal details.
  • It sends anonymous usage data (how you play) to an analytics service in the EU.
  • The data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

What the app collects

Each installation generates a random anonymous identifier on your device. It is not linked to your name or any account. Using that identifier, the app records:

DataDetails
App activityWhen the app is opened or returns from the background; which missions you start and clear; each attempt you make (the maneuver you set, the outcome, your score and the mission's budget and par); how you used the on-screen controls.
App and device informationPlatform (Android or iOS), operating system and version, device manufacturer and model, whether the device is an emulator, app version and build, update identifier, language and time zone.
Install sourceOn Android, the install referrer supplied by Google Play (for example which invite link was used to install the app).
First launch timeThe date and time of your first launch.

As with any internet request, the analytics server receives your device's IP address when the data is sent.

Your game progress (best scores, cleared missions) and a local activity log are stored only on your device.

Why it is collected

To understand whether the game is understandable and enjoyable, which missions are too hard or too easy, and to fix bugs. My legal basis under the GDPR is my legitimate interest in improving the app.

Who processes it

Analytics are processed by PostHog, using its EU cloud (eu.i.posthog.com), so the data is stored in the European Union. PostHog acts as my processor. See posthog.com/privacy. I do not share the data with anyone else and do not sell it.

How long it is kept

Usage data is kept for as long as it is useful for improving the app, and in any case no longer than needed for that purpose.

Your choices and rights

The app currently has no in-app switch to turn analytics off. Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for access to, or deletion of, data about you. Because the data is tied to a random identifier and not to your identity, I may need details such as your device model and roughly when you installed the app to locate it. Email me at the address below.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
